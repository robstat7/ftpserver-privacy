Privacy Policy

FTP Server - WiFi File Share

Published by Nijnam Apps • Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FTP Server - WiFi File Share handles information when you use the app. The app is published by Nijnam Apps ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

2. Files you share

The app lets you share files from your device over your local network by running an FTP server. Your files stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

  • We do not store your shared files on our servers.
  • We do not sell or lease your personal files.
  • File transfers happen directly between your device and connected devices on the same local network.

3. Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that may collect and process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob for advertising.
    Google advertising privacy information
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for managing ad consent choices in regions where consent is required.
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and app stability diagnostics.
    Firebase privacy and data handling information

4. Crash reporting

If the app crashes or behaves unexpectedly, Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash logs, stack traces, device information, and related diagnostic data to help us identify and fix problems.

5. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. In regions where required, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent before ad requests are made. AdMob and its partners may collect or use information such as device identifiers, IP address, and ad-related activity to provide and improve advertising services.

6. Permissions

The app uses file access and network permissions to provide FTP file sharing functionality. Files and folders remain on your device and are shared directly with devices that connect to your FTP server over the local network.

7. Data sharing

We do not share your personal files with third parties. Third-party SDKs used in the app may process limited device and diagnostics data according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data retention

We do not keep your shared files on our servers. Any crash or diagnostic data collected by third-party services is retained according to those services' own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated version will be posted on this page.
